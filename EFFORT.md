## Superset config settings touched

Two settings carry the integration: `HTML_SANITIZATION_SCHEMA_EXTENSIONS` (allow `<iframe>`) and
`TALISMAN_CONFIG` (one added key, `content_security_policy["frame-src"]`). The remaining assignments
in `superset_config.py` (`SECRET_KEY`, the two database URIs, `WTF_CSRF_ENABLED`, `FEATURE_FLAGS`,
`HTML_SANITIZATION`, `TALISMAN_ENABLED`, `TALISMAN_CONFIG["force_https"]`) exist so the demo boots
on plain HTTP against Postgres; an existing deployment most likely has them already.

Produced by the last two sections of `scripts/effort.sh`.
